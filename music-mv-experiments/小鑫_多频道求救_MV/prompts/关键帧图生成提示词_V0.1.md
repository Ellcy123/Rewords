# 关键帧图生成提示词 V0.1

## 使用方法

每次生成都同时附上 `references/` 下三张人物参考图。脸部以白衬衫参考图和三视图为身份依据，服装只采用末日丧尸猎人角色卡。先生成 S01、S05、S10、S13 四张校准图，通过后再生成其余画面。

> 16:9 cinematic photorealistic film still, the same young East Asian man Xiaoxin from the provided identity references, consistent black textured hair, brown eyes, earrings and facial beauty mark. Wardrobe must match the apocalypse hunter reference exactly: worn charcoal work shirt, tactical suspenders, black cargo trousers, combat boots, fingerless gloves, bandage on the left forearm.
