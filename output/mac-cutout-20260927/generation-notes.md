# macOS 抠图对照素材

两张原图均由内置 image_gen 生成；背景保留不透明，交给 macOS 原生工具移除。

## 简单图提示词

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a square 1024x1024 realistic studio photo for evaluating macOS background removal. One single bright red matte wooden toy rocket standing upright with three chunky fins, a simple blue circular window, fully visible centered with generous margins. Opaque solid pale cyan seamless background, crisp clean continuous edges, sharp focus, even diffuse studio light, almost no cast shadow. Deliberately easy foreground separation. No other objects, no text, no watermark. Do NOT remove the background and do NOT make it transparent.

## 复杂图提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate a square 1024x1024 photorealistic image for a difficult foreground extraction test. A single shaggy long-haired gray and tan terrier dog sitting full body in a dense garden, centered and entirely in frame, including paws and tail with generous margins. The dog has abundant extremely fine flyaway fur and long thin whiskers, semi-backlit translucent edge hairs. Gray brown dried grasses, leafy plants and small twigs fill the background with a very similar color and fine texture to the fur; thin grass blades cross slightly in front of paws. Detailed and in focus, natural daylight, readable subject but genuinely difficult boundaries and fur gaps. No collar, no other animals, no text, no watermark. Keep the actual garden background opaque. Do NOT cut out subject or create transparency.
